  • Fair comparison: Are baselines trained and evaluated under comparable conditions?

  • Sim / real gap: Which claims are supported on real hardware versus simulation only?
  • Benchmark independence: Is the evaluation public/standard or internally constructed by the authors/company?
  • Metric validity: Does the chosen metric actually represent task success or deployment usefulness?
  • Claim-to-evidence match: Which claimed contributions are strongly supported, partially supported, or still speculative?
