Template · Deep Research

Purpose. Use this as the canonical starting point for in-depth technical research posts. The report should separate source-backed evidence from inference, connect theory to deployment constraints, and end with concrete engineering recommendations and experiments.

Document metadata

  • Topic
  • Research question
  • Author / Owner
  • Audience
  • Reference date / Last updated
  • Scope / Exclusions
  • Confidence: High / Medium / Low

Executive summary

State the core conclusion, strongest evidence, engineering or strategic recommendation, and the most important unresolved risk. A reader should be able to make a preliminary decision from this section alone.

1. Problem definition

1.1 Background

Explain why the topic matters and what decision or technical uncertainty motivates the research.

1.2 Research questions

List explicit questions that the report will answer.

1.3 Terminology and assumptions

Define important terms, operating assumptions, constraints, and exclusions before comparing methods.

1.4 Evidence taxonomy

  • [Paper evidence] — peer-reviewed paper or preprint result.
  • [Official documentation] — project, vendor, repository, or product documentation.
  • [Company claim] — company announcement/demo/benchmark not independently verified.
  • [Inference] — analysis derived from the available evidence.
  • [Experiment proposal] — a test proposed to resolve uncertainty.

Use these labels inline whenever evidence strength changes how a conclusion should be interpreted.

2. Theory and technical foundations

  • Core definitions
  • Equations and variable definitions
  • Compute complexity, latency, and memory
  • Conditions under which the theory breaks in a real system

8. Risks, gaps, and open questions

Separate known risks, missing evidence, unresolved implementation gaps, and research questions.

9. Conclusions and implications

Answer the original research question directly, distinguish evidence-backed conclusions from inference, and state what should happen next.

Decision: Adopt / prototype / investigate further / reject — with a brief rationale and confidence level.

References

Use numbered references and link directly to the primary paper, official documentation, repository, benchmark, dataset, or other source used in the analysis.
